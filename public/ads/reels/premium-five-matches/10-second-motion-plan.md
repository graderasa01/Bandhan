# BandhanTak Premium 10-Second Motion Poster

## Format

- Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920
- Duration: exactly 10 seconds
- Visual character: calm, selective, premium and trustworthy
- Use the supplied poster as the final composition and animate its layers gently

## Timeline

### 0.0–2.2 seconds — The Problem

Start on the deep wine background. Fine gold threads descend slowly from above. The five profile cards are still outside the frame.

Reveal the first line with a soft opacity fade:

**हज़ारों प्रोफ़ाइल नहीं।**

Sound: one restrained low piano note with a faint silk movement.

### 2.2–5.8 seconds — The Difference

Exactly five ivory profile cards descend and settle at slightly different depths. Each card swings by only two or three degrees, like a very slow pendulum. Do not animate faces. Add a narrow champagne-gold light sweep across the card edges.

Reveal:

**रोज़ सिर्फ 5 चुने हुए रिश्ते।**

Sound: five very soft chime notes, not five loud impacts.

### 5.8–8.0 seconds — The Reason

Slowly push the camera forward by three percent. Keep the cards moving almost imperceptibly.

Reveal:

**हर मैच के पीछे एक साफ़ वजह।**

Optional voice-over:

**“हज़ारों प्रोफ़ाइल नहीं… रोज़ सिर्फ पाँच चुने हुए रिश्ते।”**

### 8.0–10.0 seconds — Brand and Action

Dim the cards slightly. Bring in the original BandhanTak mark and wordmark with a soft gold glow, followed by the CTA button.

Final copy:

**BandhanTak**

**मुफ़्त प्रोफ़ाइल बनाएँ**

**bandhantak.com**

Hold the final frame steadily until the video ends. Do not add a hard cut or loud logo sound.

## Motion Rules

- No fast zoom, flash transition, spinning card or particle explosion.
- Card swing must remain between two and three degrees.
- Use ease-in/ease-out motion and a slow three-percent camera push.
- Keep all text still after it appears so it remains readable on a small phone screen.
- Add the exact text and real logo in the editor, not inside an AI-video generation prompt.
- If using an AI video tool, animate only the background and cards. Composite typography and the logo afterward.
