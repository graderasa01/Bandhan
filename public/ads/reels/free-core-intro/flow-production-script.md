# BandhanTak — “Start With Trust”

## Production format

- Platform: Google Flow
- Format: vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920
- Duration: 32 seconds
- Language: visual directions and on-screen headings in English; every spoken line and the complete voice-over in natural Indian Hindi
- Audience: Indian adults and families beginning a serious matrimony search
- Tone: calm, modern, trustworthy and premium; no wedding glamour or exaggerated promises
- Characters: preserve the same adult daughter and father from the supplied clean plates in every generated shot

## Core product truth

The ad deliberately says “finding a match is free,” not “every possible action is free.” Creating a profile, receiving up to 15 daily matches, sending 60 monthly interests, Advanced Search, photos after adding an approved photo of your own, kundli and kundli PDF, six family seats, and up to 10 daily Grio questions are shown as free. A member pays only when choosing to open a mutual-match chat, or for another optional paid service.

The trust claim is equally precise: AI never invents missing profile details. Verification badges name what was checked, and items that were not checked stay visible. Never say that BandhanTak guarantees a person, a marriage, or that every profile has passed every check.

## Voice direction

- Narrator: warm Indian female voice, approximately 28–35 years old; calm, sincere and conversational
- Accent: natural Indian Hindi; never use an American or British Hindi accent
- Delivery: everyday Hindi, not newsreader Hindi and not an overexcited sales voice
- Pace: 0.92× normal commercial pace, with a short pause after every important number
- Pronunciation: **BandhanTak = बंधनतक**, **Grio = ग्रियो**, **AI = ए-आई**
- Speak only the Devanagari lines supplied below. Do not translate or improvise extra dialogue.

## Edit, sound and safety notes

- Music: restrained piano with one warm tanpura-like texture; no wedding dhol, fanfare or sentimental swell.
- Sound design: a quiet swipe in shot 1, a soft mic-open tone in shot 2, one confirmation tick in shot 3, and a light two-note brand resolve at the end.
- Transitions: slow dissolves or match cuts only. No flash, spin, particle burst or rapid zoom.
- Do not let Flow render the logo, plan numbers, UI labels or verification words. Composite the supplied finished overlays afterward.
- Keep faces natural and consistent. Do not add a groom, wedding scene, garland, rings or a claim that a match or marriage is guaranteed.
- Use safe margins: no important copy inside the top 120 pixels or bottom 170 pixels.
