## 0. Category ka sach

Har matrimony ad ek hi cheez bechti hai: **"sahi rishta mil jayega."** Do log,
dhoop, muskurahat, shehnai. Har brand ki ad doosre ki ad se badli ja sakti hai —
sirf logo badal do.

BandhanTak ke paas ek aisi cheez hai jo un sab ke paas nahi hai, aur wo
muskurahat nahi hai:

> Ye product **wajah likh kar deta hai** — aur wahi likh deta hai jo **pata nahi hai.**

Teenon kahaniyan isi se nikli hain. Koi bhi kahani aisi nahi hai jo kisi doosri
matrimony company ki ad ban sake — aur yahi test hai ki ad apni hai ya category ki.

---

## 1. Kahani A — "Accha ladka hai"

**Kya bech rahe hain:** har match ka likha hua reason
**Kiske liye:** 25–32 ki betiyan, aur unki maayein — dono ek saath dekhenge
**Lambai:** 35 second (Reel), 20 second ka cut-down banta hai

### Scene 1 · Shaam, ghar ka drawing room
MAA (54) sofa par. Godi me printed biodata ka dher, haath me phone. MEERA (28)
chai lekar aati hai, paas baith jaati hai.

### Scene 2 · Ek biodata, ek sawaal
Maa ek kagaz sarkati hai. *"Ye dekho."*
Meera dekhti hai. Phir poochti hai — narmi se, taane se nahi:
**"Isme accha kya hai?"**

Maa ruk jaati hai. Do second. Phir:
**"Accha ladka hai."**

Dono chup. Meera nazar milati hai, Maa nazar hata leti hai.
*Ye shot poori ad ka dil hai. Dono jaanti hain ki jawab jawab nahi tha.*

### Scene 3 · Raat, Meera akeli
Phone ki roshni. BandhanTak khula hai. Ek profile. Uske neeche — sade shabdon me,
**likha hua**:
- City preference match karti hai
- Dono ne family bonding ko priority chuna
- *(narangi me)* Relocation preference abhi unanswered hai

Meera doosri baar padhti hai. Phir teesri.

### Scene 4 · Wo uthti hai
Phone lekar Maa ke kamre tak jaati hai. Darwaze par ruk jaati hai — phir andar.

### Scene 5 · Dono ek hi screen par
Kandha se kandha. Maa aankhein sikod kar padhti hai. Bahut dheere:
**"Ye toh... likha hua hai."**

### End card
> **Har match ka reason.**
> BandhanTak · bandhantak.com

**Kyun chalegi:** ye ad ladke-ladki ki nahi, **maa-beti ki** hai. Dono ek doosre
se pyaar karti hain aur dono ke paas "kyun" ka koi shabd nahi hai. Product unhe
wo shabd deta hai. Aur last line maa bolti hai, brand nahi — isliye wo daawa
nahi, gawaahi lagti hai.

## 2. Kahani B — "Jo nahi pata"

**Kya bech rahe hain:** jo verify nahi hai wo bhi saaf likha hai
**Kiske liye:** 26–34, jinhone teen apps chhod di hain
**Lambai:** 25 second
**Risk:** sabse bold. Category me koi ye nahi kehta.

### Scene 1 · Chhat, shaam dhalte hue
RAHUL (30) akela, phone par. Profile, profile, profile. **Sab perfect hain.**
Har koi "family-oriented", har koi "fun-loving". Uska chehra khaali hai.

### Scene 2 · VO
*"Har profile kehti hai — sab theek hai."*
Wo phone neeche rakh deta hai. Sheher ki roshni.

### Scene 3 · BandhanTak
Ek match. Hari lines — verified, match karta hai. Aur phir **ek narangi line**:
> Relocation preference abhi unanswered hai

**Close-up. Wo ruk jaata hai.** Dobara padhta hai.

### Scene 4 · Chehra badalta hai
Nirasha nahi — **rahat.** Halki si hansi. Pehli baar kisi ne kuch chhupaya nahi.

### Scene 5 · VO
*"Pehli baar kisi ne ye bhi bataya — ki kya nahi pata."*

### End card
> **Jo verify nahi, wo bhi saaf likha.**
> BandhanTak · bandhantak.com

**Kyun chalegi:** har doosri app **yakeen** bechti hai. Ye **imaandari** bechti
hai — aur jis bazaar me sabko dhoka khane ka dar hai, imaandari yakeen se
zyada bikti hai. Ye ad sirf BandhanTak bana sakta hai, kyunki ye feature sach me
product me hai (`ReelPreview.tsx`, narangi wali line).

## 3. Kahani C — "Itna hi tha?"

**Kya bech rahe hain:** bol kar profile (`/bolo`)
**Kiske liye:** 45–60 ke maa-baap; betiyan share karengi
**Lambai:** 30 second
**Sabse warm — pehli ad ke liye yahi sabse safe aur sabse shareable hai.**

### Scene 1 · Raat ke 11 baje, dining table
PAPA (58), chashma, ek ungli se phone par type kar rahe hain. Lamba form.
Type karte hain. Mitate hain. Phir type karte hain.
*Chehre par koi drama nahi — bas thakan aur zid.*

### Scene 2 · Darwaze se
PRIYA (27) dekh rahi hai. Kitni der se dekh rahi hai, ye uske chehre se pata
chalta hai.

### Scene 3 · Wo paas baith jaati hai
Phone lekar BandhanTak kholti hai. Form band. Mic on.
**"Papa, aap bas bataiye."**

### Scene 4 · Papa jhijhak kar shuru karte hain
*"Naam... Priya. Beti hai meri. Dilli me hi hai..."*
Bolte-bolte awaaz khulti jaati hai. **Screen bharti jaati hai** — jaise koi sun
raha ho.

### Scene 5 · Wo upar dekhte hain
**"Itna hi tha?"**
Priya muskurati hai. Kuch kehti nahi.

### End card
> **Form nahi. Bas boliye.**
> BandhanTak · bandhantak.com

**Kyun chalegi:** ye ad shaadi ke baare me hai hi nahi — **ek baap ki mehnat**
ke baare me hai. Aur wahi cheez share hoti hai. Feature (voice profile) kahani
ka hissa hai, kahani ka maqsad nahi.

## 4. Kaunsi pehle

| | Kahani | Taakat | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pehli ad** | **C — "Itna hi tha?"** | Sabse warm, sabse zyada share hogi, feature samajhne me sabse aasan | Sabse kam — koi bold daawa nahi |
| **Doosri** | **A — "Accha ladka hai"** | Asli farq bechti hai (reason) | Dhima start; pehle 3 second mazboot chahiye |
| **Teesri** | **B — "Jo nahi pata"** | Sabse alag, sabse yaadgaar | Bold — pehle A aur C se bharosa ban jaane do |

## 5. Production ladder — "alag level" ka matlab

Teen star hain. **Sabse badi galti ye hai ki seedha teesre par paisa laga do.**

### Level 1 — Jo abhi bana hua hai · **₹0**
Code se bani: asli site ke pages, product ka apna Reel card, type, timing.
`scripts/reel/` me sab hai, ek command me nayi video.

**Isse ad mat chalao — isse *sawaal* chalao.** Chaaron hook (A/B/C/D), teeno
kahaniyon ki ek-ek line — sab is level par banao aur chala kar dekho **kaun si
jeetti hai.** ₹0 me ye pata karna is poori file ki sabse keemti cheez hai.

### Level 2 — AI scenes · **₹2,000–8,000**
ChatGPT se chehre, local GPU par lip-sync (`lipsync.py`), ElevenLabs se awaaz.

**Iska sahi istemaal:** kahani ka **animatic** banana — yaani shoot se pehle
poori ad ka kacha version, jisse pata chale ki kahani chalti hai ya nahi.
Director ko dikhane ke liye behtareen.

⚠️ **Iska galat istemaal:** ise final ad banana. Bharose wale brand me nakli
chehra pakda jaana sabse mehngi chot hai. Aur narrator kabhi ye na kahe ki wo
member hai.

### Level 3 — Asli shoot · **₹25,000–80,000** ← **yahan ye ad rehti hai**
Ek din. Do-teen actor. Ek asli ghar. Natural roshni.

**Kyun:** teeno kahaniyon ka poora vajan **chehre ke ek pal** par tika hai — maa
ka nazar hatana, Rahul ka dobara padhna, Papa ka "itna hi tha?". Koi AI model
abhi wo pal nahi banata. Aur agar bana bhi de, to jis brand ka saamaan hi
*bharosa* hai, uske liye asli chehra hamesha sasta padta hai.

**Ek din ka plan:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Crew** | Director + DOP (ek hi aadmi ho sakta hai), sound, ek assistant |
| **Kit** | Mirrorless (Sony A7/FX30 type) + 35mm aur 85mm · ek soft light + reflector · lapel mic |
| **Cast** | Maa/Papa ke liye theatre actor (asli lagte hain, model nahi), beti ke liye naya chehra |
| **Location** | Ek asli middle-class ghar — set nahi. Din bhar ke liye ₹3–8k me mil jaata hai |
| **Time** | Setup 2 ghante · Scene 1–2 me 3 ghante · Phone screens 1 ghanta · Close-ups 2 ghante |
| **Screens** | Phone par asli app chalao aur seedha shoot karo — baad me screen chipkana hamesha nakli dikhta hai |

**Ek din me teeno kahaniyan ban sakti hain** agar cast aur ghar same rakho — tab
per-ad kharcha teen guna girta hai.

## 6. Kaam karne ka tareeka

```
Level 1 par teeno kahaniyon ke hook banao        (₹0, ek din)
        ↓
Chaaron ek saath chalao, ek hafta, chhota budget  (₹3–5k)
        ↓
Jo jeette — sirf usi ka Level 2 animatic banao    (₹2–8k)
        ↓
Animatic sahi lage to Level 3 par shoot karo      (₹25–80k)
```

Is tareeke me **bada paisa tab lagta hai jab pata ho ki kaun si kahani chalti
hai** — andaze par nahi. Aur pehle do kadam itne saste hain ki galat hone par
kuch nahi jaata.

---

## 7. Jo har level par lagoo hai

- **Marriage guarantee kabhi nahi** — build plan §1 me mana hai.
- **Koi bhi rishta "yahan se hua"** tab tak nahi, jab tak asli consented couple
  na ho. Actor ki gawaahi jhooth hai.
- **Jo bhi ginti bolein, wo kisi asli plan par sach honi chahiye.** "Roz 5
  rishtey" Basic (₹999) par sach hai — `lib/constants/plans.ts` me us plan ka
  `reelPerDay` 5 hai. Bas ad ye na kahe ki ye free plan par milta hai (FREE
  par 3 hai). Koi aisa aankda mat bolna jo kisi bhi plan par sach na ho.
- **AI se bana koi bhi hissa** — Meta Ads me AI-generated toggle on, aur wo log
  kabhi member ki tarah pesh na hon.
